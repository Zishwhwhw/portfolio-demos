Ten minutes and forty-five seconds

This is the real shape of a light roast on our 15 kg drum. Every batch is logged, and if a curve drifts we roast it again.

  1. Drying, 0:00–4:30Water leaves the bean. The beans turn yellow and smell like hay.
  2. Browning, 4:30–9:10Sugars caramelise. This is where sweetness and body come from.
  3. First crack at 9:10The beans pop. We stop 95 seconds later to keep the fruit.

Questions

Can I pause or skip a delivery?

Yes. Skip one delivery or pause for as long as you like from your account. Changes made before Sunday 20:00 apply to the next roast.

Whole beans or ground?

We send whole beans by default, because ground coffee loses aroma within days. If you have no grinder, we grind for the method you chose above.

What if I don’t like the coffee?

Write to us within 14 days and we will send a different lot for free. Tell us what you did not like and we will adjust your profile.

Do you ship outside Poland?

For now only within Poland: InPost lockers for free, or a courier for 15 zł.
