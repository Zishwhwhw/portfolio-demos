Questions owners ask us

Do my staff need to install an app?

No. They get shifts in Telegram, WhatsApp or email and can confirm or swap from there. A web page works on any phone too.

Can it follow contracts and labour rules?

Yes. You set contracted hours, maximum shift length and rest time between shifts. Shiftwell won’t suggest a rota that breaks them.

What happens after the free trial?

Nothing is charged until you add a card. If you don’t, your account pauses and your rotas stay saved for 90 days.

Can I export hours for payroll?

Every month you can download worked hours per person as CSV or Excel, ready for your accountant.
