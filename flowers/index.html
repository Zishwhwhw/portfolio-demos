A flower shop inside Telegram

Mak is a fictional florist in Warsaw. Customers open the shop from the bot, pick a bouquet, write a card and choose a delivery window without leaving the chat.

  • Follows the user’s Telegram theme, light or dark
  • Telegram MainButton, BackButton and haptic feedback
  • Same-day slots close at 14:00, sold-out windows are disabled
  • Checkout through Telegram Payments, order alert to the florist’s chat
