How a Brace pizza is made

Nothing here is complicated. It just takes time, and we do not skip any of it.

  • 48h
    The dough rests for two days
    Italian type 00 flour, water, salt and very little yeast. Slow fermentation makes the crust light and easy to digest.
  • 485°
    The oven burns beech wood
    The floor reaches 485 °C. The crust puffs up and chars in spots, and the middle stays soft.
  • 90s
    Each pizza bakes for 90 seconds
    The pizzaiolo turns it three times. Then it goes straight into a vented box so it does not steam on the way.
