Booking a salon visit without leaving Telegram

A Telegram Mini App for a beauty salon. The client picks a service, a master and a free time, and the bot confirms the visit, tells the salon and sends a reminder two hours before.

  • Free times are calculated from opening hours, service length and the master’s existing visits
  • The bot checks the time again on the server, so two people can’t book the same slot
  • Uses the Telegram theme, MainButton and haptic feedback
  • Backend: Python, aiogram 3, SQLite, tests with pytest

Lumi is a fictional salon. Portfolio demo by Andrii Drymchenko. Bot source code · More demos
